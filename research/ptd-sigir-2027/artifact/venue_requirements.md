# SIGIR 2027 full-paper requirements snapshot

- Accessed: 2026-09-25 JST
- Official call: https://sigir2027.org/pages/submit-full.html
- Venue: the 50th Annual ACM SIGIR Conference, San Jose, California, July 2027.
- Full-paper format: English PDF, current ACM two-column `sigconf` format.
- Review manuscript length: at most 9 pages including all content except references.
- Required LaTeX declaration: `\\documentclass[sigconf,natbib=true,anonymous=true]{acmart}`.
- Review: double-anonymous through OpenReview; an abstract must be registered first.
- Required metadata: ACM CCS concepts and keywords.
- Artifact caveat: links that reveal author identity can cause desk rejection; use an anonymous artifact mirror.
- Dates displayed by the official call are explicitly labelled **PROPOSED**: abstract 2027-01-14, paper 2027-01-21, notification 2027-04-05, camera-ready 2027-04-30 (AoE).

This snapshot is a planning aid, not a substitute for re-checking the official call immediately before submission.
